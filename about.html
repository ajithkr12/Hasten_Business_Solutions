About Hasten

Our Story

Built to Handle the Details Businesses Don't Have Time For

Hasten Business Solutions LLP works with businesses that need dependable support across accounting, compliance and day-to-day operations, but don't want to manage several vendors to get it. We bring virtual accounting, taxation, GST, corporate compliance, auditing, financial reporting and trademark support together under one accountable team — so business owners can spend less time coordinating paperwork and more time running their business.

Every engagement is handled by people who treat your business the way they'd want their own handled — accurately, on time and without surprises.
